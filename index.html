American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB) Grading Policy

Standard uniform grading framework, quality points, and academic regulations.

Numerical Marks (%) Letter Grade Grade Point (GP) Qualitative Assessment Academic Standing & Progression Notes
90% – 100% A+ 4.00 Outstanding Dean's List / Highest Academic Distinction eligibility.
85% – 89% A 3.75 Excellent Magna Cum Laude benchmark range.
80% – 84% B+ 3.50 Very Good Cum Laude benchmark range.
75% – 79% B 3.25 Good Strong foundation, eligible for graduate assistantships.
70% – 74% C+ 3.00 Satisfactory Satisfactory academic standing.
65% – 69% C 2.75 Pass Passing grade for undergraduate coursework.
60% – 64% D+ 2.50 Passing Marginal pass; recommended candidate for retake.
50% – 59% D 2.25 Conditional Passing Lowest undergraduate passing grade.
0% – 49% F 0.00 Failing No credit earned. Course must be repeated.

Key Mathematical Formulas:

Term Grade Point Average (GPA): GPA = Σ (Credits × Grade Points) / Σ Credits
Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA): CGPA = Total Quality Points / Total Credits Earned
